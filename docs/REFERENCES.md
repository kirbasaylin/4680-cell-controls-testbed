# Primary references

Consulted 2026-09-05. These references explain the integration and review context;
they do not validate the supplied models or certify a design.

- [Siemens S7-PLCSIM Advanced function manual](https://support.industry.siemens.com/cs/attachments/109989787/s7-plcsim_advanced_function_manual_en-US_en-US.pdf) — vendor simulation context; compatibility must be checked against your version.
- [Beckhoff EtherCAT distributed clocks](https://infosys.beckhoff.com/content/1033/ethercatsystem/2469118347.html) — actual synchronization is managed by the EtherCAT master.
- [Beckhoff EtherCAT overview](https://www.beckhoff.com/en-en/products/i-o/ethercat/) — network and FSoE context.
- [UL: short-circuit current ratings](https://www.ul.com/resources/short-circuit-current-ratings-combination-motor-controller-components) — UL 508A Supplement SB method context.
- [UL: panel certification scope](https://www.ul.com/resources/does-ul-certified-industrial-control-panel-certification-cover-equipment-it-controls) — panel certification and controlled equipment have distinct scopes.
- [NFPA 79, 2024 publication](https://link.nfpa.org/all-publications/79/2024) — electrical equipment of industrial machinery; full normative review not performed.
- [IEC 60204-1:2016 scope](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/26037) — machinery electrical equipment scope; applicability and amendments require review.
